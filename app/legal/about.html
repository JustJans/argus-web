Argus Web

About

What it is

A public list of engineering and technical jobs in Europe that you filter on your own device. You describe your profile once, get a code, and the code shows you your list, on any device, with no account. The matching rules are those of Argus, a job-search bot built for one engineer and now shared.

What it is not

Not an employer, not an agency, not a job board that hosts adverts: every offer belongs to the site it links to, which is where you apply. Not a business: there are no accounts, no fees and no advertising.

Honest limits

The pile only holds what its sources allow. Spain is covered through public employment services for now, which list a small part of the private market; Sweden's national feed is large and classified by occupation. More countries and sources are added as their terms permit.

Source code, issues and the full list of sources: github.com/JustJans/argus-web.
