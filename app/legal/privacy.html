Privacy

What this site does with your data

Your CV never leaves your device. When you choose a file on the first page, it is read by a script running in your browser, and what it says about your occupations, degrees and languages ticks the filters. It is not uploaded, not stored, and gone when you leave.

Your code holds your filters, not you. Occupations, countries, posted date, level and years, languages, degrees, title words, deal-breakers. It contains no name, contact or text from your CV. It lives after the # of the address, which browsers never send to servers.

Nothing is stored in your browser. No cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no advertising scripts.

What is unavoidable. The pages and the offer files are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own access logs (your IP address, the pages requested) under GitHub's privacy statement. Every offer links to the site it lives on; what those sites do is theirs, and this site sends them no referrer.

Who runs this. A personal, non-commercial project by the maintainer of Argus. Questions and removal requests: open an issue.
