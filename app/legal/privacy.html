Privacy

What this site does with your data.

Your CV is read in your browser. It is not uploaded or stored.

Your code holds your filters, nothing else. It stays in the address bar, which browsers never send to servers.

No cookies, no storage, no analytics, no ads.

Pages are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own access logs. Every offer links to the site it lives on, without a referrer; what those sites do is theirs. A personal, non-commercial project by the maintainer of Argus. Questions or removals: open an issue.
