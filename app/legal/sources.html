    The rule

    Only sources whose written terms allow a third party to republish their listings are read: public employment services with open-data licences, and company boards on applicant-tracking systems with a documented public API. Every offer links to the page it lives on, with only a short excerpt shown here. Nothing is taken from sites whose terms forbid it.

    A source that no longer wants to be listed, or an employer who wants an advert removed: open an issue; it is acted on within 72 hours.
