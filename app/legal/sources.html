The occupations, years, degrees and languages are read with the matching rules of Argus, a job-search bot built for one engineer.

Towns are put on the map with GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), and occupations named with ESCO, © European Union.

Only sources whose written terms allow republishing are read: public employment services with open-data licences, company boards on applicant-tracking systems with a documented public API, job boards whose API asks for a link back, and employers' own careers pages, read the way search engines do (their sitemap and the JobPosting block they publish for search engines), with robots.txt obeyed. Every offer links to the page it lives on: this site shows its title, the employer and the place, never the advert's own text. To have a source or an advert removed, open an issue; it is acted on within 72 hours. Adverts that reach the site through an intermediary's partner programme (Adzuna, Jooble, Talent.com, WhatJobs) are shown in a section of their own, labelled, and link to the intermediary's page as those programmes require; the site sends them nothing about you.
